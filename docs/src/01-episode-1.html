01Episode Overview

A broken phone call pulls Sora home to Battambang for her uncle's funeral. Left alone to keep the night vigil over his coffin, she discovers that Uncle Sok did not die of illness: he tried to break an ancestral pact with a land spirit, and the spirit is now inside the house.

1.1Episode goal

Survive the funeral vigil until dawn, uncover why Uncle Sok died, and find and burn the Bronze Token, the first of the pact's five seals, before 3:00 AM.

1.2Role in the series

IntroducesMovement and interaction, the satchel and sketchbook, the kerosene lamp, timed upkeep (incense), hiding, the "moves when unwatched" scare, and the first counter-ritual.
RevealsThe 1910 pact between Sora's great-grandfather and a Neak Ta, and Uncle Sok's failed attempt to break it.
Leads toA line of blood on Sora's map points to the village pagoda, the setting of Episode 2.

1.3Visual identity

A 1930s photograph come to life: film grain, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, sepia-tinted shadows, polished teak, reflective wet mud and the restless shadows of oil flames.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Two kinds of time appear below: playtime (how far into the episode the player is) and story time (the clock in the game world).

Prologue · The Call in Phnom Penh

Playtime 0:00 – 0:25 · Story 8:00 – 8:15 PM

Narrative

Sora is working late on an illustration commission while a tropical storm rages outside. A sudden, inexplicable dread grips her. Her hands tremble and ink spills across her sketchbook. A candle on the shelf goes out, and the wall clock stops dead at 8:12 PM.

The brass wall telephone rings sharply. Through heavy static, her grandmother's voice is panicking:

"Sora… you must come home right now… Uncle Sok… he broke it… it's inside the house—"Yay Mey, over the telephone

A screech cuts through the line, then shattering glass and heavy thuds. Sora drops the earpiece, which hangs from its cord, hissing dead static.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Controls tutorialLook, walk, pick up and inspect objects, open drawers, answer the telephone.
Starting kitCollect the leather satchel, sketchbook, brass matchbox and kerosene hurricane lamp before leaving.
First sketchbook entryThe ink-spilled page becomes the sketchbook's first entry and teaches the player how to open it.

Act I · Arrival and the Vigil

Playtime 0:25 – 1:15 · Story 11:30 PM – 1:00 AM

Narrative

After an overnight train and a horse-drawn carriage through the storm, Sora reaches her family's village at 11:30 PM. The driver refuses to take the flooded road, so she walks the last stretch through the banana groves by the light of her hurricane lamp.

In the courtyard stands the white funeral tent in front of the family stilt house. Yay Mey greets her in tears. Exhausted after two days of mourning, she asks Sora to keep the late-night watch over Uncle Sok's body and gives her three sacred duties:

  1. Keep the lamp burning: never let the hurricane lamp run out of kerosene.
  2. Keep the incense alight: the four incense sticks at the corners of the coffin must never burn out.
  3. Keep the thread whole: the protective white cotton thread around the tent must stay unbroken.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Kerosene managementThe lamp's oil gauge drains steadily. The player searches the house and grounds for spare oil bottles.
Incense loopEvery 10–12 minutes the incense burns down. Fresh sticks come from the altar inside the house. If they burn out, the light dims and whispers grow louder.
Diegetic sketchbookClues, maps and symbols are drawn into the sketchbook automatically as Sora investigates.
Thread checksThe player can walk the tent's perimeter to inspect the thread, which builds tension for the moment it snaps in Act III.

Act II · The Unbroken Taboo

Playtime 1:15 – 2:30 · Story 1:00 – 2:00 AM

Narrative

At 1:00 AM, a cold gust blows out the oil lamps under the tent. Heavy, wet footsteps echo across the floorboards of the house above.

Upstairs, Uncle Sok's door stands ajar. Behind a loose wall plank, Sora finds pages from his 1931 journal. They reveal that he did not die of illness. He attempted a forbidden ritual to break a pact that Sora's great-grandfather made with a land guardian spirit, a Neak Ta, in 1910. Sok failed to bind the spirit, and it struck him down.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Gramophone puzzleThe brass-horn gramophone starts playing a funeral chant backwards on its own. Adjusting its speed dial reveals a spoken clue to where the desk-drawer key is hidden (see section 6.1).
HidingDragging footsteps approach in the hallway. Sora must put out her lamp and hide behind a wooden wardrobe until the shadow passes the doorway.
The altered portraitBack downstairs, all four incense sticks have turned to white ash, whatever the player did. In the portrait on the altar, Uncle Sok's eyes are now open and staring at Sora.

Act III · The First Seal

Playtime 2:30 – 3:30 · Story 2:00 – 3:00 AM

Narrative

A violent snap echoes across the yard: the main protective thread has broken in two. The oil lamps go out one by one and the darkness creeps inward. Inside the coffin, Uncle Sok's body shifts position whenever Sora turns her back.

Using the translated journal pages, Sora realises she must find the Bronze Token of the Neak Ta, locked away in the house, and perform a counter-ritual before 3:00 AM.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Under-house searchIn the pitch-black area under the house, Sora tracks a roaming shadow by 3D sound while collecting three brass key fragments hidden inside clay water jars.
Unlocking the chestUpstairs, the assembled key opens the iron-bound chest. Inside: the Bronze Token (Seal #1) and a map with four other locations marked across Cambodia.
Counter-ritual and climaxSora places the token in the main incense burner and lights it with sanctified matches while reciting the counter-mantra. The entity appears directly behind her, its huge shadow thrown across the white tent cloth. She must finish the lighting sequence without turning around.

Resolution and Cliffhanger

Story about 3:00 – 4:30 AM

As the token burns, a blast of wind tears through the white tent. The entity lets out an echoing screech that fades into the night. Quiet returns, and in the last hours before dawn the first grey light rises over the banana groves.

The curse on the house is broken for now, and Uncle Sok's body settles peacefully. But when Sora opens her sketchbook, a fresh line of dark blood bleeds across the map, pointing straight to the village pagoda and setting up Episode 2: The Whispering Pagoda.

05Mechanics Reference

Values marked target are starting points to tune during playtesting.

MechanicRulesFeedback to the player
Kerosene lampBurns oil continuously while lit. A full tank lasts about 12 minutes (target). Spare oil bottles refill it. The lamp can be turned off to save oil or to hide.Glass gauge on the lamp; the flame shrinks and sputters when low.
MatchesLimited supply in the brass matchbox. Needed to relight the lamp, candles and incense, and for the final ritual (sanctified matches).Count visible when the matchbox is opened.
Incense loopThe four corner sticks burn down every 10–12 minutes. Replacing all four resets the timer. Letting them burn out darkens the tent and raises whispering and entity activity until they are replaced.Stick length; smoke thinning; whispers.
Protective threadStory-driven in this episode: it breaks at a scripted moment in Act III. Inspecting it beforehand is optional but builds tension.Visual state of the thread; the snap sound.
HidingStanding in a hiding spot (behind a wardrobe or in deep shadow) with the lamp off makes Sora undetectable unless the entity bumps into her.Muffled sound, heartbeat, darker view.
Watched / unwatchedThe corpse (and later the entity) moves only when it is outside the player's view.Changed position when the player looks back.
"Do not turn around"During the final ritual, turning the camera past a set angle fails the sequence and restarts it from its checkpoint.Shadow on the tent cloth; breathing behind the player.
Being caughtA short scare, fade to black, and a restart at the last checkpoint. There is no health bar.Screen distortion, sound, torn sketchbook page.

06Puzzle Walkthroughs

6.1The gramophone

StepActionResult
1Enter Uncle Sok's room; the gramophone starts playing a reversed chant by itself.Unsettling sound; points the player to the machine.
2Inspect the gramophone and find the governor speed dial.Dial becomes interactive.
3Turn the dial until the warped audio becomes clear speech.A spoken clue reveals where the desk-drawer key is hidden.
4Retrieve the key and open the desk drawer.Further journal pages and a translation aid.

6.2The brass key and the chest

StepActionResult
1Read the journal: the chest key was broken into three pieces and hidden under the house.Objective sketched in the sketchbook.
2Search the clay water jars under the house while avoiding the roaming shadow.Three brass key fragments, one per jar.
3Combine the fragments in the satchel.Complete key.
4Remove the loose wall planks in Uncle Sok's room and unlock the iron-bound chest.Bronze Token (Seal #1) and the map of four locations.

6.3The counter-ritual

StepActionResult
1Carry the token to the coffin altar before 3:00 AM.Ritual begins; checkpoint saved.
2Place the token in the main incense burner.The entity appears behind Sora.
3Strike sanctified matches and light the burner while the mantra is recited, without turning around.The token burns; the seal breaks.

11Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes from v1.0

  • Reorganised into a standard structure with a mechanics reference, puzzle walkthroughs, entity and item tables and a glossary.
  • Added story-clock times to every act alongside playtime.
  • Khmer text now renders correctly (it appeared as empty boxes in v1.0).
  • "Sunlight… as 4:00 AM approaches" changed to "first grey light". Sunrise in Cambodia is around 5:30–6:00 AM.
  • Defined a fail state (checkpoint restart, no health bar) and target values for oil and incense.

Open issues

Decide before building Episode 1
  • The pact's history: Episode 2 shows an offering at a forest altar while Episode 4 describes a contract signed at a rubber estate. The journal pages here must match whichever version is chosen.
  • Date of the pact: large rubber estates in Kampong Cham mostly date from the 1920s, so 1910 may need adjusting.
  • Sora's parents and Yay Mey's place in the bloodline are never explained, though the curse runs for "four generations".
  • The map's "four other locations": consider making them faint marks revealed one at a time by the blood trail, to preserve the mystery.
  • Source of the sanctified matches for the final ritual.
  • The locked storage shed: v1.0 says the token is "locked inside the under-house storage shed" but then retrieves it from the chest upstairs. Here the token is in the chest. Give the shed a purpose (for example, oil bottles or a lore note) or remove it.
  • Hidden lore notes and cross-episode tracking for the Episode 5 endings.
