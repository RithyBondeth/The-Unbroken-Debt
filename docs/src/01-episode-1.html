01Episode Overview

A broken phone call pulls Sora home to Battambang for her uncle's funeral. Left alone to keep the night vigil, she learns that the family owes a debt to a land spirit, that it collects on the seventh night after Sok's death, and, in the last minutes before dawn, that the name written for the final payment is hers.

1.1Episode goal

Keep the three vigil duties, discover why Uncle Sok died, and burn the Bronze Token, the first seal, before 3:00 AM.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Each act shows playtime (minutes into the episode) and story time (the clock in the game world).

Prologue · The Call

Playtime 0:00 – 0:10 · Night 1, 8:00 – 8:15 PM

Sora works late on an illustration during a storm. Dread grips her; ink spills across her sketchbook; a candle goes out; the clock stops at 8:12 PM. The brass telephone rings. Through static, Yay Mey:

"Sora… you must come home right now… Uncle Sok… he broke it… it's inside the house—"Yay Mey, over the telephone

A screech, shattering glass, the line goes dead. The player learns to move, pick up and inspect, then packs the satchel, sketchbook, matchbox and hurricane lamp. A fade covers the two-day journey by train and carriage.

Act I · Arrival and the Vigil

Playtime 0:10 – 0:40 · Night 3, 11:30 PM – 1:00 AM

Narrative

The carriage driver refuses the flooded road, so Sora walks the last stretch by lamplight. In the courtyard, under the white funeral tent, Yay Mey greets her in tears. Vuthy is there too, in a town suit, stiff and polite. He calls the rituals "superstition", tells Sora their grandmother is not well, and leaves for town before midnight, promising to return at dawn.

Exhausted after two days of mourning, Yay Mey gives Sora the three vigil duties: keep the lamp burning, keep the four corner incense sticks alight, and keep the protective thread around the tent unbroken. Before resting, she asks Sora to prepare a proper betel offering for the ancestral altar, "as your mother used to".

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Vigil dutiesLamp oil drains; incense burns down every 10–12 minutes; the thread can be inspected.
Betel offering tray newArrange the offering correctly (section 6.1). Reward: matches blessed at the altar, the sanctified matches needed for the final ritual.
Optional: storage shedFind the key in Yay Mey's sewing box. Spare oil and two lore notes.

Act II · The Unbroken Taboo

Playtime 0:40 – 1:15 · Night 3, 1:00 – 2:00 AM

Narrative

At 1:00 AM a cold gust blows out the tent's lamps. Wet footsteps cross the floorboards above. In Sok's room, behind a loose plank, Sora finds pages of his journal addressed "to whoever keeps my vigil". They describe Great-grandfather Chea's pact of 1910, the four payments, the five seals, and a warning:

"On the seventh night after my death it will come to collect what is owed. Break the seals before then. I tried to begin. If you are reading this, I failed."Uncle Sok's journal

Gameplay

ElementDescription
GramophonePlays a funeral chant backwards on its own; fix the speed to hear where the desk-drawer key is hidden (section 6.2).
Hallway walkerDragging footsteps approach. Put out the lamp and hide behind a wardrobe until the shadow passes.
The portraitDownstairs, all four incense sticks are ash, and in the portrait Sok's eyes are open, staring at Sora.

Act III · The First Seal

Playtime 1:15 – 1:50 · Night 3, 2:00 – 3:00 AM

Narrative

The protective thread snaps. Lamps die one by one. In the coffin, Sok's body shifts whenever Sora looks away. The journal says the first seal, the Bronze Token, is in the iron-bound chest, and its key was broken into three pieces and hidden under the house.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Under-house searchCollect three brass key fragments from clay jars while the roamer hunts by sound and light.
The chestAssemble the key; open the chest: the Bronze Token, a map with four faint marks, and a folded slip of paper Sora pockets without reading.
Counter-ritualPlace the token in the main incense burner and light it with the sanctified matches while the mantra is recited. The presence stands behind Sora, its shadow huge on the tent cloth. She must not turn around.

Resolution · The Fourth Name

Night 3, 3:00 AM – dawn

The token burns; wind tears through the tent; the screech fades. In the grey light before dawn, Sok's body lies still. Vuthy returns from town and asks what happened and what his father left. Key choice: show him the journal, or keep it (section 7).

Alone again, Sora unfolds the slip from the chest. In Sok's hand: a list of four names, three crossed out: Chea, Chhun, Kanha. The fourth line has been scraped and rewritten. It reads Sora.

Blood seeps across her map, darkening the first faint mark: the village pagoda. In the sketchbook, she draws seven circles and fills in three. Four nights remain.

06Puzzles

6.1The betel offering tray new

A short cultural puzzle that teaches the player to look carefully, and that the household's rituals matter.

StepActionResult
1Listen to Yay Mey's instruction and look at the mother's old drawing of the tray, pinned near the altar.Sketch of the correct layout added to the sketchbook.
2Gather betel leaves, areca nut, lime paste, a candle and incense from around the house.Items in the satchel.
3Arrange them on the tray as in the drawing and place it on the altar.Yay Mey blesses a set of matches: the sanctified matches.

A wrong arrangement can be corrected freely, but each wrong placement records a small respect penalty. The exact layout must be confirmed with the cultural advisor.

6.2The gramophone and desk

StepActionResult
1The gramophone starts a reversed chant by itself.Draws the player in.
2Turn the governor speed dial until the audio becomes clear speech.Sok's recorded voice names the hiding place of the drawer key.
3Retrieve the key; open the desk drawer.The rest of the journal, including the chest's location.

6.3The key and the chest

StepActionResult
1Search the clay jars under the house while avoiding the roamer.Three brass fragments.
2Combine the fragments in the satchel.Complete key.
3Remove the loose planks in Sok's room; unlock the chest.Bronze Token, map, folded slip.

6.4The counter-ritual

StepActionResult
1Bring the token to the coffin altar before 3:00 AM.Checkpoint saved.
2Place it in the main incense burner.The presence appears behind Sora.
3Light it with the sanctified matches without turning around.Seal #1 breaks.

07Key Choice and Tracking

Share the journal

Sora shows Vuthy his father's journal. He reads it in silence, then says he has to think. Vuthy +1. In Episode 3 he already knows what the seals are, which makes his later actions a deliberate choice rather than ignorance.

Keep the journal

Sora says only that his father left old papers. Vuthy nods and doesn't push. No change. He learns the truth on his own later, and resents being kept out.

12Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes in v3.0

  • Playtime reduced to 1.5–2 hours; the journey is a fade; the act structure is tighter.
  • New: Vuthy, the betel offering puzzle (which supplies the sanctified matches), the seven-night deadline, threat escalation, the optional storage shed, the dawn key choice and the "fourth name" revelation.
  • Sok now dies trying to burn the first seal himself, which explains Yay Mey's "he broke it".
  • The Bronze Token is in the chest (the shed is now an optional area); the map shows faint marks revealed one by one.

Open issues

Confirm with the cultural advisor
  • The correct contents and layout of a household betel offering, and who would bless the matches.
  • Customs for the night vigil and for the seventh-day funeral rites.
  • Khmer spellings of all names and terms in the glossary.
