01Episode Overview

The photograph leads Sora to the Tonle Sap and the clinic where her mother died. The episode opens in afternoon light among the living, then the monsoon arrives. Inside the drowned clinic, Sora learns why her name is on the list, and who the spirit in the water really is.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Floating Market formula break

Playtime 0:00 – 0:25 · 4:30 PM – 11:00 PM

For the first time, the player is somewhere full of life and daylight. Sora moves between boats asking about the old clinic. Most people go quiet; a noodle seller points her to an old fisherman who "rowed a woman there once".

Vuthy is waiting at the market. He is pale and jumpy. "Grandmother told me something. Please, Sora, leave it alone. Let it end the way it was written." If Sora shared the journal in Episode 1, he knows exactly what the seals are and begs her to stop; if not, he won't say why. She refuses to leave.

Pa Lok knows her face: she has her mother's eyes. He tells her he rowed Kanha to the clinic in 1920 and never forgave himself. As the storm rolls in at dusk, he lends her a boat:

"The water remembers everything, girl. The spirit in that clinic drags down anyone who steps into her dark water. Keep yourself dry, and keep your light bright."Grandfather Pa Lok

At 11:00 PM, Sora rows into the storm.

Act II · Whispers Under the Floorboards

Playtime 0:25 – 1:05 · 11:30 PM – 2:30 AM

Water-damaged ledgers show that Sok worked here as the clinic's medic until 1920 and returned in secret in 1931. As Sora wades into the wards, the water ripples with no wind. The Chhlong Tonle rises from an opening in the floor, a long-haired shape made of water, and sinks again.

ElementDescription
Aquatic stealthWatch for ripples; climb onto furniture, crates and upper walkways; use the wall lamps' safe zones.
SealingBoard windows and cover roof leaks with planks and tarps to stop key rooms flooding.
FusesThree dry brass fuses in sealed medical tins restore power to the drainage pump.
Pich newSmall footsteps on dry boards and a child's giggle lead Sora along safe routes above the water and to the hardest-to-find fuse. Following him is optional but helpful.

Act III · The Letter in the Safe

Playtime 1:05 – 1:45 · 2:30 – 4:30 AM

The generator roars; the pump drains the basement. The iron safe's dial uses Khmer numerals. Sok's logs give one date in the Khmer lunar calendar, the night of Kanha's death, which Sora must convert (section 7.2).

Inside: the Terracotta Water Jar, sealed with earth from the 1910 shrine, and a bundle of unsent letters from Sok to his son. As Sora lifts the jar, the vault dissolves into a playable memory as Sok, kneeling in this same room in 1931 (section 5). When it ends, the water around her has gone still, and the spirit rises in front of her, close enough now for Sora to see her mother's face.

Then the Neak Ta strikes: stilts crack and the clinic tilts toward the lake. Sora climbs out of the flooding basement on shifting boards to the upper deck, where she must perform the purification while the spirit circles in the water below (section 8).

Resolution · Pa Lok's Raft

4:30 – 5:00 AM

Sora runs for her boat. It is gone; the rope has been cut. With the clinic collapsing, she escapes across the roofs to the last standing stilt (section 7.4). Pa Lok, who couldn't sleep, finds her there at first light.

On the raft he admits that a young man in a town suit paid him that afternoon to "keep the girl away from the clinic" and asked where her boat was tied. Sora knows who it was. Before she leaves, Pa Lok presses his old brass compass into her hand: "Where you're going, you'll need to know which way is home."

Inside the jar's seal, wrapped in oilcloth, is a burnt piece of rubber stamped Compagnie des Caoutchoucs de Kampong Cham, 1928, the year Sok visited the estate. Blood seeps from the stamp, east across the map. Sora fills in the fifth circle. Two nights remain.

05The Mid-Season Twist

Playable memory · Sok, 1931

The player becomes Sok, kneeling in the dry vault in 1931 with a rubbing of the slab's fourth line before him. He cuts his palm and mixes his blood with earth from the jar. The player must scrape away the name Vuthy and write Sora; the action cannot be refused, only slowed. Sok's hand shakes. He whispers: "Forgive me, Kanha. She's yours. Let her be yours, and let my boy live." The memory ends with Sora holding the jar.

Sok's letters (optional reading)

Sok writes to Vuthy that he failed to save Kanha in this clinic, and that the fourth payment would take Vuthy, the eldest of the fourth generation. He could not bear it. In 1931, using earth from the 1910 shrine sealed in the terracotta jar, he performed a ritual that scraped Vuthy's name from the debt and wrote Sora's in its place. She had no one left, he reasons, and she had left the family behind.

Then guilt: he learned that the named debtor alone can break the seals and sever the pact. He decided to begin the work himself and spare her, knowing it would probably kill him. If he failed, his journal would guide whoever kept his vigil. "She will have the way out I am giving her. It is more than I gave Kanha."

What it changesSok is not a tragic hero but a frightened father who chose Sora to die. The journal that guided her was an apology and a trap at once.
RecontextualisesThe scraped name on the Episode 1 slip; Yay Mey's silence; the photograph of Sok and Kanha; why Sok knew so much about the clinic.
Sets upVuthy's motive in Episode 4 (once he knows, he has every reason to make sure the debt is paid by Sora) and the final choice in Episode 5.

With no boat, Sora must find a route across the clinic's roofs, rope bridges and broken stilts to the one stilt that still stands. The route uses planks and ropes the player may have moved during the sealing puzzle.

08Key Choice and Tracking

Free her

Sora stays at the rail and calls her mother by name, with the full verse from the doctor's quarters notes (or Pa Lok's words) and one more candle. The water rises around her while she does it. The spirit becomes Kanha for a moment, touches her daughter's face, takes the small hand of the child spirit beside her, and dissolves with him into light. Respect +2. Kanha's voice will help Sora in the Episode 5 Water trial.

Break the seal and run

Sora completes only the seal ritual. The flash disperses the spirit into painful mist, not freed, and Sora runs. Pich's footsteps fade into the water after his mother. No change. In Episode 5, Kanha's sorrow distorts the Water trial, making it harder.
