01Episode Overview

On Pchum Ben, the night the gates between the living and the dead stand open, Sora follows the blood on her map to the village pagoda. Blind hungry ghosts hunt by sound among the ancestral stupas, and the only way to the scripture library holding her family's record is through them.

1.1Episode goal

Reach the locked scripture library in the middle of the lotus pond, recover the family's palm-leaf record and the Silver Betel Box (Seal #2), and escape the pagoda before the 4:00 AM bell.

1.2Role in the series

IntroducesCrouching, surface-based footstep noise, holding breath, the lamp shutter, thrown distractions, the mirror light-beam puzzle and the timed escape.
RevealsA vision of the 1910 pact: Sora's great-grandfather offering the family's silver betel box to a spirit to save his family from starvation.
Leads toA 1925 photograph of Uncle Sok at a floating clinic on the Tonle Sap, the setting of Episode 3.

1.3Visual identity

Cold moonlight blue, mossy grey stone, flickering lotus oil cups, fog drifting over the lily ponds and pitch-black shadows between the stupas.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Gates of Pchum Ben

Playtime 0:00 – 1:00 · Story 11:45 PM – 12:30 AM

Narrative

Sora arrives at the village pagoda on a creaking bicycle at 11:45 PM. It is the height of Pchum Ben (ភ្ជុំបិណ្ឌ), the night the gates between the spirit world and the human world open. The grounds are deathly quiet under a low mist.

In the monks' quarters she meets Venerable Sophal, the blind head monk. He tells her that Uncle Sok came to the pagoda a year ago to study forbidden spirit-binding texts. To break the second seal, Sora must retrieve her family's palm-leaf manuscript from the locked scripture library over the lotus pond. He gives her a blessed white cotton thread for protection and a warning:

"Tonight, the hungry spirits walk among the ash stupas. They have no sight, but they hear every breath. Do not step into their path, and do not waste the rice offerings."Venerable Sophal

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Crouching and stealthCrouch behind stupas; footsteps are louder on dry leaves than on soft dirt; hold breath when an entity passes close.
Lamp shutterThe hurricane lamp's metal shutter now closes, hiding the light instantly without putting out the flame.
Rules of the nightThe monk's warning is recorded in the sketchbook as the episode's taboos.

Act II · The Wandering Shadows

Playtime 1:00 – 2:30 · Story 12:30 – 2:30 AM

Narrative

In the stupa garden, tall, gaunt Pret patrol the paths, drawn by the scent of funeral offerings. The gate to the lotus-pond bridge is sealed with a bronze lock bearing four symbols.

To open it, Sora must find the four stupas that belong to her family line, marked with the family emblem drawn in her sketchbook, and place the correct offering in front of each: sticky rice balls, lotus buds or sanctified water. All the while, she must avoid the Pret.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Spatial audio stealthPret give off raspy, high-pitched breathing. Players track them around stone corners by 3D sound.
Offering puzzleClues from Uncle Sok's journal and the family inscriptions tell which offering belongs at which ancestor's stupa (see section 6.1).
DistractionsSora can throw small clay rice bowls to make noise and lure Pret away from narrow passages.

Act III · The Scripture Archive and Seal #2

Playtime 2:30 – 3:30 · Story 2:30 – 4:00 AM

Narrative

With the gate open, Sora crosses the rickety bridge to the library. Among shelves of palm-leaf manuscripts she finds her family's record box.

Opening the cabinet triggers an interactive flashback: in 1910, her great-grandfather kneels before an ancient stone altar and offers a sacred family relic, a silver betel box, to a woodland spirit in exchange for saving his family from starvation.

The vision shatters as the Neak Ta shadow slams against the library's wooden walls, and the whole building groans on its stilts over the pond.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Moonlight mirror puzzleRotate the polished bronze mirrors to bounce a beam of moonlight from the roof window onto the silver lock of the central cabinet (see section 6.2).
Seal #2The cabinet holds the Silver Betel Box, engraved with ancient guardian runes.
Escape sequenceThe entity breaks through the roof beams and the shelves collapse. Sora must cross the collapsing bridge on a timer while shadow tendrils reach out of the water.

Resolution and Cliffhanger

Story 4:00 AM

Sora reaches the safety of the main archway as the first bell of 4:00 AM rings, driving the Pret and the shadow back into the darkness.

Inside the Silver Betel Box she finds a dried, pressed water-lily leaf wrapped around a photograph from 1925. It shows Uncle Sok standing on a floating wooden clinic on the Tonle Sap lake. A fresh line of blood seeps from the photograph's edges, pointing to Episode 3: The Drowned Village.

05Mechanics Reference

New mechanics in this episode. Everything from Episode 1 still applies. Values marked target are starting points for playtesting.

MechanicRulesFeedback to the player
CrouchLowers the camera, slows movement and roughly halves footstep noise (target). Stupas block line of sight and sound.Lower view; softer footsteps.
Surface noiseEach ground surface has a noise level: dry leaves are loud, stone is medium, soft dirt and moss are quiet.Distinct footstep sounds per surface.
Hold breathSilences Sora's breathing for a few seconds (target 6 s), then a recovery gasp that is loud if she holds too long.Muffled sound; heartbeat; gasp.
Lamp shutterThree lamp states: open, shuttered (flame still burning and still using oil, no light) and out. Shuttering is instant; relighting costs a match.Shutter position visible on the lamp model.
DistractionThrown clay rice bowls make a noise where they land. Pret investigate the sound. A limited number are available.Smash sound; Pret turn toward it.
Rice-offering tabooWasting or knocking over rice offerings counts as a broken taboo and attracts nearby Pret.Pret shriek; noted in the sketchbook.

11Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes from v1.0

  • Reorganised into the standard episode structure; added mechanics, puzzle, entity, item and glossary sections.
  • Added story-clock times to every act.
  • Khmer text now renders correctly.

Open issues

Decide before building Episode 2
  • No counter-ritual for the Silver Betel Box. Episodes 1, 3 and 4 each end by burning, purifying or melting their seal, but this episode only retrieves it. Episode 5 treats all four seals as destroyed and needs a "Silver Betel Box inlay". Suggestion: a short dawn ritual at the family stupa that releases the inlay.
  • The flashback and Episode 4 must agree on where and how the 1910 pact was made (forest altar vs. rubber estate contract).
  • Gap between episodes: make clear that this is the following night.
  • Pchum Ben timing: the festival lasts 15 days; decide which night this is (the final day, Pchum Ben itself, is the natural choice).
  • Monk's title: confirm the correct Khmer form of address for a head monk.
