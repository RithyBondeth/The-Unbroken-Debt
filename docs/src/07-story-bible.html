01Logline and Theme

Logline

A young woman who abandoned her family comes home for a funeral and discovers that her uncle has signed her life over to an ancient land spirit in his son's place. She has seven nights to break the pact, or pay it.

1.1Theme: what we owe the land and each other

Everyone in this story takes something they have not paid for. The game asks whether a debt can be ended by giving back rather than by sacrificing someone else.

Who takesWhat they takeHow the story answers it
Chea (1910)His children's lives and a fortune, from the guardian of the landHis descendants pay with their lives.
The French company (1923)The forest, the shrine and the labour of its workers, without honouring any of themBy 1932 the estate is failing and lost in fog: the land is taking it back.
Sok (1931)Sora's life, to save his son'sIt costs him his own life, and leaves Sora the burden.
Sora (before 1932)Her freedom, by abandoning her family and its pastShe must come home and carry what she ran from.
Vuthy (1932)His safety, at Sora's expenseHe must choose to stand beside her and accept the risk.

The true ending is the only outcome in which nobody takes from anybody: Sora and Vuthy give their own blood, and the family gives back the fortune that was never theirs.

02The Rules of the Debt

These rules are fixed. Every scene, puzzle and ending must obey them.

#RuleDetail
1The priceIn return for saving Chea's children and giving the family prosperity, the guardian takes one life from each of four generations of Chea's bloodline. People who marry in (Yay Mey, Sora's father) are not of the bloodline.
2The scheduleEach payment is taken when the guardian chooses. The fourth falls due one full twelve-year cycle after the third: Kanha died in 1920, so it falls due in 1932.
3The namesThe name of each person to be taken is written on the Golden Inscription Slab at the guardian's seat on Phnom Kulen. The fourth name was originally Vuthy, the eldest of the fourth generation.
4Changing a nameA name can be changed by a ritual using earth from the shrine where the pact was made and the blood of the person making the change. Whoever does it loses the guardian's patience: the next offence against the pact will kill them. Sok did this in 1931.
5The sealsFive consecrated objects bind the pact. Only the named debtor can break them. Anyone else who tries breaks a rule of the pact. Sok tried on Night 1 and died.
6The seven nightsAn attempt on the seals wakes the guardian and calls the debt due. It will collect on the seventh night, the night of the seventh-day funeral rites, at dawn. Before then it is bound by its own contract and will not take the named debtor.
7Other spiritsThe guardian does not command the Pret, the drowned spirit or the forest mimics. They are drawn by the festival of the dead, by grief and by the wakened debt, and they can kill Sora (the fail state).
8The paid dead are boundEach person taken stays bound to the place they died until the pact ends: Kanha at the clinic, Chea and Chhun as memories in the guardian's seat. They can be released early by a full rite of release.
9The gateThe waterfall gate on Phnom Kulen opens only for the named debtor. Once all four elemental shrines are lit, the sanctum is open to anyone of the bloodline, which is how Vuthy reaches the altar.
10SeveranceWith all five seals broken, the named debtor may sever the pact at the altar: thread and incense at the four corners, the true counter-mantra, and her own name redrawn on the slab in ink mixed with her blood, freely given.
11JudgementThe guardian accepts the severance only if the bloodline has shown respect: honouring the dead, keeping taboos, telling the truth. If it is not satisfied, it treats the offering as a payment instead.
12The unsatisfied guardianWhen it treats the offering as a payment, it takes the first name written for the fourth generation: Vuthy's, still faintly visible under Sora's. This is the bitter ending, and the reason Vuthy is afraid.
13Shared bloodIf two of the last generation redraw their names together, freely, the debt is closed for the whole line and nobody is taken.
14Taking backWhen the pact is severed, "the guardian takes back what it gave": the family's land and fortune return to the earth. If it is fulfilled by a death, the family keeps everything.
15FailureIf the counter-mantra is broken (too many wrong verses), the severance fails outright and the guardian takes the named debtor, Sora. This is the bad ending.

03Characters and Arcs

3.1Sora (សូរ៉ា), 24

WantsTo survive, end the curse and go back to her life in Phnom Penh.
NeedsTo stop running from her family and accept that she belongs to it.
FlawShe abandoned them. After her father's death in 1929 she stopped answering Yay Mey's letters, missed every memorial for her mother, and told herself the village and its beliefs had nothing to do with her. A bundle of those letters, unopened, sits in her studio drawer in the prologue.
GiftShe draws what she cannot say. Her mother Kanha drew too; the betel-tray drawing in Episode 1 is Kanha's. The sketchbook is how Sora understands the world, and in the finale she redraws the contract with her own hand.
ArcOutsider (Ep 1) → listener (Ep 2) → betrayed daughter (Ep 3) → alone and angry (Ep 4) → the one who gives back (Ep 5).
Defining line"I spent years pretending none of you were mine. I'm not doing that tonight."

07Consistency Checklist

Question a player might askAnswer
Why can't Sok break the seals himself?Only the named debtor can (rule 5).
Why seven nights, and why now?The fourth payment is due in 1932 (rule 2); Sok's attempt called it due on the seventh night (rule 6).
Why doesn't the guardian just take Sora early?It is bound to collect only on the appointed night (rule 6).
Then what can kill Sora before Night 7?The other spirits (rule 7).
Why is Vuthy afraid if he is no longer named?An unsatisfied guardian takes the first name written (rule 12).
How does Vuthy reach the altar?The sanctum opens to the whole bloodline once the shrines are lit (rule 9).
Why does the family lose its fortune in the true ending?Severance takes back what was given (rule 14).
Why is Kanha's spirit at the clinic?The paid dead stay bound where they died (rule 8).
Why isn't Yay Mey in danger?She married in and is not of the bloodline (rule 1).
