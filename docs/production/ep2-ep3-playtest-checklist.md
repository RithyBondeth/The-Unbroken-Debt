# Episodes 2 and 3: your playtest checklist

The things only a person can judge. Automated checks cover loading, story steps, routes and saves, but they can't hear the sound or tell you whether it's scary. Wear headphones and allow about 30 minutes per episode. Note anything wrong and where it happened.

## Start each episode

Start the dev server, then open:

- Episode 2: `http://localhost:5183/?start=ep2&profile=ep2-play`
- Episode 3: `http://localhost:5183/?start=ep3&profile=ep3-play`

## Episode 2: sound

- [ ] Footsteps sound right on each surface you walk on, including the kmaoch's dragging leg.
- [ ] The monks' chant comes from the direction of the hall and gets louder as you approach.
- [ ] The corrupted chant is unsettling, not funny.
- [ ] Whispers and steps seem to come from behind you.
- [ ] The heartbeat rises with fear and doesn't drown out everything else.
- [ ] The forest drum, gong, sobs and knocks in the walls feel spaced out, not constant.
- [ ] The red ghost's neck cracks and laugh line up with what you see.
- [ ] The apparition's scream startles you without being painfully loud.

## Episode 2: play

- [ ] The kmaoch walks and lurches naturally, and its head and body look like one creature.
- [ ] The red ghost's motion is natural, and she flickers in and vanishes cleanly.
- [ ] The "behind" scare works when you turn round.
- [ ] Stupas and statues look like old stone up close, with no blocky textures or camouflage patches.
- [ ] The naga heads at the gate are recognisable, and the lamps stand beside them.
- [ ] The family stupas' cloth and plaques look right, and the lamps don't blow them out.
- [ ] The game feels smooth, especially at the gate.
- [ ] The new zones are worth exploring, and you can't get lost for too long or stuck. (Once built.)

## Episode 3: sound

- [ ] Rain on tin and the water fill the space, and the storm builds.
- [ ] Market birds and ambience stop feeling safe at the right moment.
- [ ] Oar strokes match your A and D presses.
- [ ] The straining timber and breaking piles make the building feel unsafe.
- [ ] Underwater breathing reads clearly.
- [ ] The drainage pump is audible and doesn't become annoying.
- [ ] Kanha's lullaby is unsettling and clear.

- [ ] The ending, with the candles and the mother's choice, lands emotionally.
