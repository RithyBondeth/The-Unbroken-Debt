01Episode Overview

The rubber stamp leads Sora to a colonial estate in Kampong Cham, built over the forest where her great-grandfather made the pact. Vuthy is waiting at the gate, asking to help. In the fog, the spirits call her name in the voices of her family, his voice included, and before the night is over Sora must decide whether the cousin who betrayed her deserves to be saved.

1.1Episode goal

Cross the rubber grid to the manor, uncover the history of the shrine beneath the factory, open the cellar, and melt the Iron Tapping Knife, the blade Chea cut his palm with in 1910, in the coal furnace.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · Two Lanterns in the Fog formula break

Playtime 0:00 – 0:30 · 11:30 PM – 12:45 AM

At the gate, Vuthy confesses to cutting the boat and asks to come. For the first time, Sora is not alone: Vuthy walks a few steps ahead with his own lantern, talks quietly, and sometimes points out the row numbers.

The fog thickens. A voice calls behind them: Yay Mey, lost in the trees. Then Sok. Then Kanha, singing the lullaby from the clinic. Sora remembers the elders' rule:

If you hear a voice calling your name in the dark forest at night, never answer, and never turn around.Khmer folk taboo

Then Vuthy's voice calls her name from behind, while Vuthy is walking in front of her. From here on, the player can never be sure which Vuthy is real.

ElementDescription
Compass and grid mapFollow the hand-drawn map between numbered rows; wrong turns loop back in the fog.
Boundary-stone offerings newSmall offerings at the old shrine's path stones reveal the guardian's ancient route through the grid (section 6.1).
Voice-mimic tabooWhen a mimic speaks behind Sora, keep walking forward without turning or replying.
CompanionVuthy follows Sora's lead, stops when she stops, and never speaks from behind her. That is the player's only tell.

Act II · What the Overseer Wrote

Playtime 0:30 – 1:05 · 12:45 – 2:30 AM

In the manor's archive, Jean-Luc's ledgers record the clearing of the forest in 1923: workers refused to dig near a stone shrine; the old men of a nearby village told him of a man called Chea who made a bargain there in 1910; when the shrine was dug out, the workers found an iron knife crusted with old blood. Jean-Luc built the factory over the site anyway and kept the knife "as a curiosity" in the cellar. His private notebook is in cipher (section 6.2).

The decoded notebook holds the line that changes everything for Vuthy: the villagers said that if the pact is ever broken, "the guardian takes back what it gave". The land, the house, the money would all return to the earth.

While Sora repairs the factory's gear mechanism, Vuthy reads it over her shoulder and goes quiet. When the cellar doors open, he steps through first and slams them shut behind him. Sora is locked out and must find another way down through the factory's ventilation shafts.

ElementDescription
Overseer's cipher newDecode Jean-Luc's notebook using a Khmer proverb his interpreter taught him.
Gears and valvesCollect three iron gears in the manor; fit them and set the boiler valves from the schematic (section 6.3).
Manor shadowsShapes lurk in window frames and wardrobes and lunge at bright light.
Optional: barracksThe workers' own records of the shrine, and the altar they kept to appease it.

Act III · The Shrine Beneath the Furnace

Playtime 1:05 – 1:45 · 2:30 – 4:00 AM

Sora crawls through the ventilation shafts into the deepest cellar. Vuthy stands at the shrine stone holding the Iron Tapping Knife, meaning to carry it away so that the seal can never be broken. "If you pay, it ends and we keep everything. I'm sorry, Sora. I'm so sorry."

The ground shakes. The Neak Ta rises from the shrine stone in full physical force. Shadow tendrils wrap around Vuthy and lift him off the floor; the knife falls at Sora's feet. Steam pipes burst all around.

ElementDescription
Smelting ritualLoad coal, light the furnace with matches, place the knife in the crucible, work the bellows until it melts.
Pressure defenceUse the steam valves to drive back the shadows while the knife melts, and keep the lantern lit.
The choiceOne valve is aimed at the tendrils holding Vuthy (section 7).
Vent escapeThe seal breaks with a shockwave that collapses the stairs; crawl out through the collapsing ventilation tunnel.

Resolution · One Night Left

4:00 AM

Sora crawls into the wet grass as the fog lifts, the molten silver-iron ingot cooling in her hand. When she opens her sketchbook, the ingot draws the blood lines together into one path, north to Phnom Kulen, the ancient seat of the guardian, where the contract itself is inscribed.

If she saved Vuthy, he sits beside her, shaking, and asks to come: "Let me at least stand at the door." If she didn't, she finds him at dawn at the edge of the estate, silent, his eyes not quite his own. Sora fills in the sixth circle. One night remains.

StepActionResult

2Use the proverb's letters as the substitution key to decode the marked passages.The knife's location, the cellar route and the "takes back what it gave" warning.

StepActionResult

2Collect three iron gears from manor rooms; fit them as drawn.The mechanism turns.
3Set the boiler valves to the values on the drawing.The cellar doors open (and Vuthy locks them).

StepActionResult
1Load coal and light the furnace.Checkpoint.
2Place the knife in the crucible and work the bellows while venting steam at the shadows.The knife melts; Seal #4 breaks.

07Key Choice and Tracking

Save Vuthy

Sora turns the valve on the tendrils holding him. It leaves one fewer valve to defend the furnace, so the defence is noticeably harder. Freed, Vuthy grabs the bellows and works them with her. They crawl out together. Vuthy: saved (required for the true ending).

Leave him

Sora keeps every valve on the furnace. The tendrils drag Vuthy into the dark behind the shrine stone. He survives, but the guardian has marked him. Vuthy: not saved. The true ending is no longer possible.

Changes in v3.0

  • Playtime reduced to 1.5–2 hours.
  • History resolved: the 1910 pact was made at a forest shrine; the estate cleared the forest in 1923 and built the factory over it; the knife is Chea's blood-oath blade.

Open issues

  • Choose the Khmer proverb for the cipher with the cultural advisor.
  • Companion AI is new work; keep Vuthy's behaviour simple (follow, wait, light) to fit a solo budget.
