01Episode Overview

On the last night of Pchum Ben, when the dead walk among the living, Sora goes to the village pagoda for the second seal. Blind hungry ghosts hunt by sound among her ancestors' stupas. In the scripture library she lives the night of the pact through her great-grandfather's eyes, and must decide what really happened.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Gates of Pchum Ben

Playtime 0:00 – 0:25 · 11:45 PM – 12:30 AM

Sora arrives by bicycle at 11:45 PM on the last night of Pchum Ben (ភ្ជុំបិណ្ឌ). In the monks' quarters, Venerable Sophal tells her that the guardian returned Chea's silver betel box the morning after the pact, filled with earth: the sign that the offering was accepted. Yay Mey gave it to the pagoda for safekeeping in 1912. Sok tried to take it last year; the monk refused him. Tonight, he says, it belongs to the one who is named. Only the named can break a seal, and the guardian weighs the truth above everything.

"Tonight, the hungry spirits walk among the ash stupas. They have no sight, but they hear every breath. Do not step into their path, and do not waste the rice offerings."Venerable Sophal

He gives her a blessed white cotton thread. The player learns to crouch, read surfaces (dry leaves are loud, moss is quiet), hold breath and close the lamp's new shutter. Across the pond, a single Pret drifts past, too far to hear her: a lesson, not a threat.

Act II · The Hungry Garden

Playtime 0:25 – 1:05 · 12:30 – 2:15 AM

The gate to the lotus-pond bridge is sealed with a bronze lock of four symbols. Sora must find her four family stupas, belonging to Chea, his wife, Chhun and Kanha, and place the right offering at each. Every offering she places feeds the dead, and draws more of the hungry.

ElementDescription
Stupa offeringsMatch each ancestor to the right offering using inscriptions and the journal (section 6.1).
DistractionsThrow clay rice bowls to pull Pret away from narrow passages.
Temple bells new · optionalStrike the prayer hall's bells in the order of a verse painted on its wall to release the chains (section 6.2). Inside: two lore notes, including an old villager's account of what Chea asked for.

Act III · The Archive and the Memory of 1910

Playtime 1:05 – 1:45 · 2:15 – 3:45 AM

Sora crosses the bridge to the library. A beam of moonlight from the roof window must be guided across bronze mirrors to the silver lock of her family's cabinet (section 6.3). Inside lie the family record and the Silver Betel Box. As she opens the record, the room dissolves.

Flashback · Chea, 1910 formula break

It opens on a memory of the harvest the year before: by firelight, villagers dance a slow folk dance, Chea's children laughing among them. The music falters, the fire dies, and the same village is empty and starving. The player becomes Chea: old hands, a guttering oil lamp, a village of empty houses and coughing children. He walks the forest path to the Neak Ta shrine, kneels and offers the silver betel box. A voice like grinding stone asks what he wants. The player chooses his words (section 7). The forest goes silent; the box grows heavy with earth.

The vision shatters. The Neak Ta shadow slams into the library walls; the building groans on its stilts.

Escape

The roof splits; shelves collapse. Sora races across the breaking bridge as shadow tendrils reach from the black water.

Resolution · The Dawn Offering

3:45 – 4:00 AM

Sora kneels at Chea's stupa as the 4:00 AM bell rings. She opens the betel box, pours its old earth at the stupa's foot, lights incense and speaks the names of her ancestors. The silver inlay on the lid comes loose in her hand; Seal #2 is broken. The Pret withdraw into the mist.

A photograph slips from the box's lining: Tonle Sap clinic, 1920. Uncle Sok in a medic's white coat stands on a floating wooden clinic; beside him, smiling and heavily pregnant, is Sora's mother. Blood seeps from its edges onto the map, darkening the second mark. Sora fills in the fourth circle. Three nights remain.

07Key Choice and Tracking

At the shrine, the guardian asks Chea what he wants. The family remembers a humble plea; the village remembers greed. The lore notes, especially the villager's account in the optional prayer hall and Chea's own confession in the family record, reveal the truth: he asked for both.

Chea's words (player choice)Result
"Take what you will. Only save my children."The family's version. The vision flickers. No change.
"Give us land and riches, and our line is yours."The village's version. The vision flickers. No change.
"Save my children… and let us never be poor again."The truth. The vision holds steady. Respect +1. The guardian values honesty.

12Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes in v3.0

  • Playtime reduced to 1.5–2 hours.
  • New: the playable 1910 flashback and honest-memory choice; the temple bell puzzle and optional prayer hall; Pret escalation tied to offerings; the dawn offering that breaks the seal (fixing the v1 gap); the 1920 photograph of Sok and Kanha (replacing the 1925 photo).
  • The pact now happens at a forest shrine in Kampong Cham, consistent with Episode 4.
  • v3.1 (Story Bible): the monk states rules 5 and 11 (only the named can break a seal; the guardian values truth).
  • v3.2: the flashback opens with a short memory of a harvest dance, to make the famine's loss felt. Dance is shown as joy and memory, never as a ghost.

Open issues

Confirm with the cultural advisor
  • Appropriate offerings at family stupas during Pchum Ben, and whether a lay person would pour earth or speak names there.
  • Whether temple bells could plausibly be struck in sequence like this, or whether a drum or gong fits better.
  • The correct Khmer form of address for a head monk.
