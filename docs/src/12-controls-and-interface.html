01Interface Goals

GoalMeaning
Diegetic firstInformation lives on objects Sora holds or sees: the lamp's gauge, the sketchbook, the matchbox. No health bar, minimap, crosshair or objective marker.
Few, honest exceptionsSubtitles, a save indicator, first-time control hints and accessibility options are allowed on screen (section 6.3).
Simple controlsEvery action has one key. New actions arrive one per episode, never several at once.
Tension stays onOpening the satchel or sketchbook does not pause the game (an accessibility option can change this).
Readable in two languagesAll interface text in English and Khmer, using Ubuntu and Preah Vihear.

03Hands and Interaction

RangeObjects within 1.8 m at the centre of the view can be used.
Focus feedbackNo crosshair. A focused object gets a faint pencil-line outline (like Sora sketching it) and Sora's right hand lifts slightly towards it. A soft paper tick sound plays the first time an object is focused.
UsingShort actions happen on press; continuous actions (dials, bellows, pouring) follow the mouse or stick while the key is held.
Reading documentsPicking up a document brings it close to the camera. A transcript in the chosen language can be shown beside it (on by default), so handwritten Khmer or French is always readable.
Cannot useIf an action is impossible, Sora's hand hesitates and pulls back; the sketchbook may gain a hint the second time.

04The Satchel

Tab · openCamera tilts down; the satchel opens on Sora's lap or held in front of her
BrowseItems lie physically inside: lamp oil, matchbox, keys, letters, seal remnants
TakeClick: item moves to the right hand
InspectRight-click: rotate it close up
CombineDrag one item onto another
  • The game keeps running while the satchel is open; footsteps and breathing remain audible, so opening it near a threat is a risk.
  • Item counts appear physically (several bottles, the number of matches when the box is opened). A small handwritten label appears only when an item is inspected.
  • Combining fragments (Ep 1) shows them clicking together in Sora's hands.

05The Sketchbook

Section (ribbon tab)Contents
MapSora's map of the current area, drawn as she explores; the Cambodia map with blood marks; the seven night circles.
NowCurrent objectives as small drawings with a few words (for example the three vigil duties).
NotesSummaries of documents and recordings; puzzle clues copied by Sora (the betel drawing, the gramophone dial, the safe date).
PeopleQuick portraits of characters with a line about each, updated as the story changes.
LoreCollected lore notes, with an empty outline for each one not yet found in the episode.
  • New entries: a quiet pencil-scratch sound and a short shift of Sora's hand to her satchel. No pop-up.
  • Page turning with A/D or the mouse; tabs selectable directly.
  • Text on pages is drawn large enough to read at 1080p; a "large text" setting increases it further.

06Communicating Without a HUD

6.1Player state

StateHow the player knows
Lamp oilGlass gauge on the lamp (raise the lamp to read it clearly); the flame shrinks and sputters below 25%.
Matches leftVisible when the matchbox is opened in the satchel.
NoiseFootstep sounds by surface; Sora's breathing grows louder when hurrying.
HiddenSound muffles slightly; vignette deepens; heartbeat slows.
Threat nearbySpatial sound of the entity; Sora's breathing and heartbeat quicken; slight vignette pulse.
Wet and cold (Ep 3)Water-streak vignette at the screen edges; shivering breath; unsteady view; lamp flicker.
Shadow damage (Ep 5)Screen darkens and desaturates; sound distorts; recovers slowly.
Taboo brokenA distinct low sound and a short entry in the sketchbook.

6.2Progress

EventHow the player knows
Objective updatedNew "Now" page drawing, with a pencil sound.
Checkpoint reachedA page-turn sound and the save indicator (6.3).
Being caughtShort scare, fade, a torn page, restart.
Episode completeSummary page in the sketchbook: lore found, respect symbols, the night count.

6.3Permitted on-screen elements

ElementRule
SubtitlesBottom centre, maximum two lines, optional speaker names and background.
Save indicatorA small lamp icon in the top-right corner while saving. Important on the web, so players don't close the tab mid-save.
Control hintsShown the first time an action is needed, as pencil text that fades; never repeated once performed (can be re-enabled).
Accessibility aidsInteraction dot, sound captions and directional threat indicator, only when enabled.

09Settings and Accessibility

CategoryOptions
GraphicsQuality preset · render scale · field of view (60–95°) · brightness calibration · film grain · chromatic aberration · head-bob · motion blur off by default
AudioMaster · ambience · effects · voice · music · headphone mode (sharper 3D positioning)
LanguageInterface: English / Khmer · Subtitles: English / Khmer / off · Voice: Khmer
SubtitlesSize (small, medium, large) · background opacity · speaker names · sound captions (e.g. "[dragging footsteps, above]")
ControlsMouse sensitivity · invert Y · rebind keys · crouch and hold-breath as toggle or hold · gamepad layout and vibration
AccessibilityInteraction dot · directional indicator for threat sounds · document transcripts · pause while satchel or sketchbook is open · reduced lightning flashes · large sketchbook text · story mode (slower, less perceptive spirits)
Why these matter

The game relies heavily on sound and darkness. Sound captions, the threat indicator and brightness calibration make it playable for deaf and hard-of-hearing players and on poor screens without changing the experience for others.

10Browser Behaviour

SituationBehaviour
Mouse captureClicking the game requests pointer lock. Browsers always release it on Esc, so losing pointer lock opens the pause menu; this is why Esc cannot be rebound.
Audio startAudio unlocks on the first click (the main menu's Start button), as browsers require.
Tab hidden or window unfocusedPause the game and mute audio; resume to the pause menu, not straight into play.
FullscreenA button in the menu and settings; F11 also works. The game also runs well windowed.
Closing the tabIf a save is in progress, the browser's "leave site?" warning is shown.
Unsupported deviceWithout WebGPU, on mobile, or on integrated graphics, show the requirements page (minimum and recommended hardware, supported browsers) instead of a broken page.
Keyboard layoutsBindings use physical key positions so they work on non-QWERTY layouts.
