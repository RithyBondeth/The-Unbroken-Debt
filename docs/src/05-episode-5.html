01Episode Overview

On the seventh night, Sora climbs Phnom Kulen to the guardian's ancient seat. Four trials beneath the mountain each end in a memory of one generation the debt has taken. At the altar, with the guardian towering over her, she performs the Ritual of Severance and decides what to do with the cousin who wanted her to pay.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Mountain and the Four Gateways

Playtime 0:00 – 0:30 · 2:30 – 3:30 AM

Sora climbs the Naga staircase of Phnom Kulen (ភ្នំគូលេន) through tropical mist, following the ingot's pull. If Vuthy was saved, he climbs beside her, and at the waterfall he stops: an unseen force will not let him pass. "Only the named," Sora says. If he was not saved, she hears steps behind her all the way up and never sees who it is.

An optional side path follows a stream over ancient carvings, where verses in the stone match the funeral chants (section 6). Behind the waterfall lies the Central Rotunda. Sora places each remnant in its niche:

Act II · The Four Generations

Playtime 0:30 – 1:30 · 3:30 – 5:00 AM

The trials can be played in any order. Each tests a skill from an earlier episode and ends with a memory of one generation of the debt.

Act III · The Ritual of Severance

Playtime 1:30 – 2:15 · 5:00 AM – sunrise

With four shrines lit, the rotunda's stone doors open onto the Sanctum of the Banyan Tree. On the altar lies the Golden Inscription Slab, the contract of 1910, with four names, three of them faded. The fourth reads Sora, with the ghost of another name scraped out beneath it.

The ground trembles and the Neak Ta Guardian rises over the altar. Sora cannot fight it. She must perform the Ritual of Severance while it attacks (section 6). As the final phase begins, Vuthy is there: with all four shrines lit, the sanctum has opened to the whole bloodline.

05Threat Escalation

Because the trials can be played in any order, escalation is tied to how many are complete, not which.

Trials completeWhat changes
0 (first trial)Each trial at its base difficulty.
1The guardian's presence enters the rotunda; the hub is no longer safe to linger in.
2Trials gain an extra hazard: more statues, one more Pret, faster fire traps, extra ripples.
3The light falters: oil drains faster and braziers gutter.
4 → SanctumThe full manifestation and the three-phase ritual.

06The Ritual of Severance

PhasePlayer actionsPressure
1 · The Four CornersRun to the four corner pillars; tie the blessed thread (from Ep 2) around each; light sanctified incense at each.Area attacks and tendrils between pillars.
2 · The ChantsAt the altar, choose each verse of the counter-mantra from lines written in the sketchbook. Lore notes from every episode reveal which lines are true; without them, some choices are guesses.Each wrong verse causes shadow damage and counts as a chant error.
3 · The OfferingPrick a finger with the brass pin, mix the blood with ink from her fountain pen, and redraw her own name on the slab with her own hand. Vuthy steps forward (section 7).The final choice.
Shadow damage

There is no health bar. The screen darkens, sound distorts and Sora's breathing grows ragged. After two or three hits (target) the phase restarts from its checkpoint. Chant errors are counted even when the phase restarts.

How lore unlocks the chants

The counter-mantra has six verses. For each, the sketchbook offers three candidate lines copied from different sources. Lore notes across the series (journal pages, Chea's confession, the clinic records, the overseer's notes, the riverbed carvings) confirm which line is genuine. A player who found most of the notes can complete the chants with certainty; one who found few must guess.

07Endings

7.1The final choice

Forgive him

Sora holds out her hand. Vuthy adds his blood to the ink, and Sora writes both their names on the slab together. The debt was written on the whole bloodline; two of its last generation now answer it together.

Refuse him

Sora turns away and draws her name alone. Vuthy stands at the edge of the light, the guardian's shadow falling over him.

7.3Outcomes

True ending · The Unbroken Freedom

As the last stroke of their two names dries, every name on the slab burns away. Golden light pours from the banyan. The guardian lowers its arms, bows its masked head and dissolves into drifting dust. Through the open dome, the sky turns gold. Sora and Vuthy walk out into the sunrise together. In Battambang, Sok's cremation fire burns under a clear sky. The rice fields will flood and fail next season, the fortune is gone, and Yay Mey holds both grandchildren and says it was never theirs to keep.

Bitter ending · The Last Payment

The slab takes Sora's drawn name, but the guardian is not satisfied: the contract is fulfilled, not severed. Its shadow falls over Vuthy and the fourth name on the slab changes back to his. He is gone when the light returns. Sora walks into the sunrise alone. The family keeps its land and money, and at Sok's cremation she stands apart, knowing what they cost.

Bad ending · Bound to the Soil

The counter-mantra breaks apart. The guardian rejects the severance and declares the debt unpaid. Darkness swallows the sanctum. Sora wakes inside the coffin in the funeral tent in Battambang, unable to move or speak, as her family weeps over her body on the eighth morning.

Changes in v3.0

  • Playtime 2–2.5 hours. Each trial ends with an ancestor's memory; escalation is tied to trials completed; remnant-to-shrine mapping defined.
  • Lore notes now unlock the true chant lines; the optional riverbed carvings added.
  • Vuthy's final choice and three endings with exact conditions (replacing the undefined "key lore choices").
  • The Guardian's mask is no longer called "Mukhalingi" (a mukhalinga is a carved linga, not a mask).
  • v3.1 (Story Bible): Phase 3 is now Sora redrawing her name in ink and blood (her art matters at the climax); Vuthy enters because the sanctum opens to the bloodline (rule 9).
Open issues
  • The blood offering and the linga/yoni altar are sensitive; review with the cultural advisor and keep the presentation restrained.
  • The riverbed carvings evoke real sacred sites near Phnom Kulen; keep the in-game version fictional and respectful.
  • Name the Guardian's mask with an accurate term.
  • Playtest the respect threshold (6 of 9) and the chant-error limit (3).
