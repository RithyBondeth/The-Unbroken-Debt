01Episode Overview

The 1925 photograph leads Sora to the Tonle Sap in the middle of a monsoon. She rows to an abandoned French-Khmer clinic on stilts, half swallowed by floodwater and haunted by the spirit of a woman who drowned there. In this place the water itself is the enemy.

1.1Episode goal

Reach Uncle Sok's hidden vault in the flooded clinic, restore power to the drainage pump, recover the Terracotta Water Jar (Seal #3) from the iron safe and purify it before the clinic collapses into the lake.

1.2Role in the series

IntroducesRowboat navigation, wetness and body temperature, safe-zone lights, sealing windows and leaks, an enemy that moves only through water, and the fuse and generator puzzle.
RevealsUncle Sok used the abandoned clinic as a hidden sanctuary in the 1920s and stored a relic in its safe.
Leads toA burnt piece of rubber stamped with a 1928 colonial company seal, pointing to Kampong Cham and Episode 4.

1.3Visual identity

Cold dark teal, murky brown floodwater, glistening wet wood, rain streaking the view, lightning-flash shadows and warm amber lamplight reflected on black water.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Monsoon Surge

Playtime 0:00 – 1:00 · Story 1:30 – 2:15 AM

Narrative

Following the photograph, Sora travels by horse cart to the edge of the Tonle Sap (ទន្លេសាប), arriving at 1:30 AM in a fierce monsoon. The lake heaves with surging waves.

She hires a small rowboat from Grandfather Pa Lok, an old fisherman. He warns her that the stilt clinic has stood abandoned since a tragic medical accident in 1920, and that no fisherman goes near it after midnight:

"The water remembers everything, girl. The spirit in that clinic drags down anyone who steps into her dark water. Keep yourself dry, and keep your light bright."Grandfather Pa Lok

Sora rows through the storm toward the dark shape of the clinic, creaking high above the water.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Rowboat navigationThe player rows a small wooden boat between floating structures, with water physics affected by waves and wind.
Wetness and warmthWading through floodwater slows Sora down and soaks her clothes. If she stays wet too long, her body temperature drops and her lamp flickers dangerously.
Drying lampsLarge fixed lamps on the clinic walls dry Sora and create temporary safe zones that repel the water spirit.

Act II · Whispers Under the Floorboards

Playtime 1:00 – 2:30 · Story 2:15 – 3:45 AM

Narrative

On the clinic's lower deck, Sora searches for Uncle Sok's old storage vault. Water-damaged ledgers reveal that Sok used the abandoned clinic as a hidden sanctuary in the 1920s and locked a rare relic in the iron safe below.

In the flooded wards, the temperature plummets. The water around her ankles ripples when nothing moves it. The Chhlong Tonle appears, rising silently from openings in the flooded floor, a long-haired shape made of water.

To reach the vault, Sora must find three dry brass fuses on the creaking upper floors and restore power to the generator that runs the drainage pump.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Aquatic stealthThe spirit travels only through water and flooded floors. Players watch for ripples and climb onto dry furniture, crates or upper walkways to avoid her.
Sealing windows and leaksSora uses wooden planks and canvas tarps to seal broken windows and roof leaks, stopping rain from flooding key puzzle rooms.
Generator fusesThree dry fuses are stored in sealed medical tins. The player finds them while tracking the spirit by the slosh of wet, dragging footsteps.

Act III · The Sunken Safe and Seal #3

Playtime 2:30 – 3:30 · Story 3:45 – 5:00 AM

Narrative

Sora installs the fuses and cranks the diesel generator to life. The machine shakes the floorboards as the drainage pump starts, and the water in the basement vault begins to drop.

She descends into the muddy basement. The iron safe stands in the centre. Using dates from Uncle Sok's medical logs, she sets the combination. Inside lies the Ancient Terracotta Water Jar (Seal #3), inscribed with protective Pali runes and filled with sanctified river soil.

As she lifts the jar, the Neak Ta strikes outside, smashing the clinic's support stilts. The building tilts toward the churning lake and the floodwater rushes back in.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Timed vault escapeClimb out of the tilting, flooding basement, balancing on shifting floorboards and dodging falling debris.
Purification ritualOn the upper deck, Sora pours sanctified water over the jar while lighting three yellow candles arranged in a triangle around it.
ClimaxAs the counter-mantra is recited, a blinding flash bursts from the jar. The Chhlong Tonle dissolves into harmless mist and the waves around the clinic fall calm.

Resolution and Cliffhanger

Story 5:00 AM

Sora sits exhausted in her rowboat as the storm eases at 5:00 AM, and the pale pink of dawn spreads over the lake.

She breaks the jar's clay seal. Inside, wrapped in oilcloth, is a burnt piece of dried rubber latex stamped with a French colonial seal from 1928: Compagnie des Caoutchoucs de Kampong Cham. A drop of blood seeps from the stamp onto her sketchbook map, pointing east to Episode 4: The Rubber Plantation.

11Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes from v1.0

  • Reorganised into the standard episode structure; added mechanics, puzzle, entity, item and glossary sections.
  • In-story time changed from "2:00 AM – 5:30 AM" to 1:30 AM – 5:00 AM to match the narrative (arrival 1:30 AM, dawn 5:00 AM).
  • "Fixed kerosene spotlights" renamed "fixed wall lamps"; spotlights are unusual for 1932 (large pressure or carbide lamps fit the period).
  • Khmer text now renders correctly.

Open issues

Decide before building Episode 3
  • Clinic timeline: abandoned since a 1920 accident, yet the spirit drowned "decades ago" and Sok was photographed there in 1925. Suggestion: she drowned in 1920 (twelve years earlier), and Sok used the empty clinic as a hiding place afterwards.
  • Source of the sanctified water and yellow candles for the purification ritual.
  • Rowboat scope: full water physics is expensive for a solo project; a guided boat on a path with limited steering is the fallback.
  • Sensitivity: the spirit died in childbirth. Treat her story with care and review it with the cultural advisor.
