01Episode Overview

The colonial rubber stamp leads Sora east to a vast plantation in Kampong Cham, swallowed by an unnatural fog. In the endless rows of trees, spirits call her name in the voices of the people she loves. In the abandoned manor, she finally learns the full truth of what her great-grandfather signed.

1.1Episode goal

Cross the fog-bound rubber grid to the colonial manor, uncover the original contract, open the factory cellars and melt the Iron Tapping Knife (Seal #4) in the coal furnace.

1.2Role in the series

IntroducesThe hand-held compass, grid-map navigation, the fog loop, the voice-mimic taboo, the gear and valve puzzle, the furnace defence and crawling through tight spaces.
RevealsThe full truth: in 1910, during famine and cholera, Sora's great-grandfather, a labour supervisor on the estate, signed a blood contract with the Neak Ta, pledging his bloodline for four generations. Uncle Sok came here in 1928 to burn the contract ledger and failed.
Leads toThe molten ingot fuses the blood lines on the map into one path to Phnom Kulen, the setting of Episode 5.

1.3Visual identity

Dense grey volumetric fog, muted olive green, rusty iron orange, decaying yellow French stucco, brown dirt trails, and the warm amber sphere of lantern light.

04Story and Gameplay by Act

Act I · The Grid of Voices

Playtime 0:00 – 1:00 · Story 11:30 PM – 12:30 AM

Narrative

Sora reaches the edge of the vast Kampong Cham (កំពង់ចាម) estate late at night. An unnatural fog has smothered the region and hidden the moon. With a brass compass and her oil lantern, she enters the grid of rubber trees to find the abandoned manor.

In the silence, soft voices begin to drift through the fog. Her grandmother calls: "Sora… over here… I'm lost in the trees…" Then Uncle Sok's voice calls from another direction.

Sora remembers a taboo her elders taught her:

If you hear a voice calling your name in the dark forest at night, never answer, and never turn around.Khmer folk taboo

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Compass and map navigationThe player holds a brass compass and follows Sora's hand-drawn grid map between numbered tree rows. Wrong turns quietly loop back in the fog.
"Don't look back" tabooWhen a Khmaoch Prey speaks in a familiar voice behind Sora, a distinct sound cue plays. The player must keep walking forward without turning the camera or making a sound until the voice fades.
Lantern fuelThe lantern burns oil faster in the dense fog. Small oil cans hang at tapper stations throughout the grid.

Act II · Secrets of the Manor

Playtime 1:00 – 2:30 · Story 12:30 – 2:30 AM

Narrative

Sora escapes the grid and reaches the overgrown manor. Inside, she searches Overseer Jean-Luc's archived letters and the estate ledgers from 1910.

There she finds the dark truth of her family line. During a devastating famine and cholera outbreak in 1910, her great-grandfather was a labour supervisor on this estate. To save his dying family and secure wealth, he signed a blood contract with the ancient land guardian, pledging the spiritual devotion and freedom of his bloodline for four generations. Uncle Sok came here in 1928 to burn the original contract ledger but was driven out by the entities guarding the cellar.

To reach the cellar vault where the contract's tool is kept, Sora must solve the gear puzzle in the factory shed.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Factory gears and pipesStudy French mechanical drawings copied into the sketchbook, collect three missing iron gears from the manor and set the boiler's pressure valves to open the heavy cellar doors (see section 6.1).
Indoor evasionMove through dark, creaking corridors while avoiding shadow shapes lurking in window frames and wardrobes.

Act III · The Furnace Vault and Seal #4

Playtime 2:30 – 3:30 · Story 2:30 – 4:00 AM

Narrative

Sora descends into the latex cellars beneath the factory. The air is thick with the smell of old sulphur and dried latex. In the deepest chamber, resting in the frame of an open coal furnace, she finds the fourth seal: an Iron Tapping Knife crusted with dried blood and engraved with binding runes.

As she lifts it, the Neak Ta manifests in full physical force, blocking the cellar exit with towering shadow tendrils as the steam pipes burst around her.

Gameplay

ElementDescription
Smelting ritualLoad coal into the furnace and light it with matches. Place the knife in the crucible and work the bellows until the metal melts completely.
Pressure defenceWhile the knife melts, manage the steam valves to keep the shadows back and keep the lantern lit, under intense spatial audio.
Ventilation escapeAs the knife melts, the seal breaks with a supernatural shockwave that collapses the cellar stairs. Sora crawls through a collapsing concrete ventilation tunnel to the surface.

Resolution and Cliffhanger

Story 4:00 AM

Sora crawls out of the ventilation shaft into damp grass as the fog begins to lift at 4:00 AM. In her hand is the molten silver-iron ingot from the furnace.

When she opens her sketchbook, the ingot reacts with the paper: the blood lines on the map fuse into one, pointing far north to the sacred mountain of Phnom Kulen, where an ancient sunken temple holds the Neak Ta's central altar. The stage is set for Episode 5: The Sunken Sanctuary.

11Revision Notes and Open Issues

Changes from v1.0

  • Reorganised into the standard episode structure; added mechanics, puzzle, entity, item and glossary sections.
  • In-story time changed from "11:30 PM – 3:30 AM" to 11:30 PM – 4:00 AM to match the ending.
  • "Hand-crank lantern fuel" changed to "lantern fuel"; v1.0 mixed up a hand-crank light with an oil lantern.
  • Khmer text now renders correctly.

Open issues

Decide before building Episode 4
  • Historical date: large rubber plantations in Kampong Cham were mostly established in the 1920s. A working estate with ledgers in 1910 is early. Either move the pact to the early 1920s or make the 1910 site a forest shrine that the estate was later built over.
  • The pact must match Episode 2's flashback (an offering of the silver betel box at a forest altar). One option: the offering happened at a shrine on estate land, and the ledger recorded the "contract".
  • "Four generations": clarify who those generations are (great-grandfather, grandparent, Sok and Sora's parent, Sora), and where Sora's parents are.
  • Forced labour and colonial history should be portrayed accurately and respectfully; many plantation workers were brought from Vietnam. Review with a historian.
  • Where the compass comes from (carried over or found at the start of the episode).
